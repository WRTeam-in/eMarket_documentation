# Point of Sale (POS)

The Point of Sale (POS) screen lets sellers bill in-store customers from the seller panel. Sellers can search products, build a cart, add a customer, apply a discount or extra charges, and place the order.

---

## 1. Opening POS

Open **Point of Sale** from the seller panel menu.

![Point of Sale menu](/img/detailed-documentation/pos-menu.webp)

The **POS Billing** screen opens. Click **Exit POS** at the top left to go back to the seller panel.

---

## 2. POS Billing Screen

![POS Billing](/img/detailed-documentation/pos-billing.webp)

The screen has two sides:

- **Left side**: bill tabs and the product list.
- **Right side**: discount and charges buttons, customer details, cart and payment.

### Products

- **Search**: find a product by typing in the **Products** search box.
- **All Categories**: filter the product list by category.
- **Product card**: shows the product image, name, size, the selling price and the original price.
- **Variant dropdown**: products with more than one variant show a dropdown on the card. Select the variant before adding it.
- **Add to Cart**: adds the product to the cart.

### Customer Details

- **Search Customer**: find an existing customer.
- **Register User**: register a new customer from the POS screen.

### Cart

- Each cart item shows the product image, name, size, price and quantity.
- Use **-** and **+** to change the quantity.
- Use the delete icon on an item to remove it.
- **Clear Cart**: remove all items from the cart.
- **Subtotal** and **Total** are shown below the items. When the cart is empty, the cart shows "Cart is empty".

### Payment and Order

1. Select the payment method from **Select Payment Method** (for example **Cash**).
2. Click **Save & Print Bill** to save and print the bill, or click **Place Order** to place the order.

---

## 3. Multiple Bills (Hold Bill)

![Multiple Bills](/img/detailed-documentation/pos-multiple-bills.webp)

Use multiple bills to serve more than one customer at the same time.

1. Click **Hold Bill & Create Another** to keep the current bill on hold and start a new one.
2. Each bill gets its own tab (**Bill 1**, **Bill 2**, **Bill 3** and so on) with its own cart.
3. Click a tab to switch between bills.
4. Click the **x** on a tab to close that bill.

---

## 4. Add Discount

![Add Discount](/img/detailed-documentation/pos-discount.webp)

Click **Add Discount** on the POS screen to give a discount on the bill.

- **Discount Percentage**: enter a percentage. The current **Subtotal** is shown below the field.
- **Discount Amount**: enter a fixed amount. It must not exceed the total.
- The discount amount is applied on the total amount.
- Click **Apply Discount** to apply it, **Clear Discount** to remove it, or **Cancel** to close the form.

---

## 5. Add Additional Charges

![Add Additional Charges](/img/detailed-documentation/pos-additional-charges.webp)

Click **Add Additional Charges** to add extra charges to the bill, for example a packing charge.

1. Enter the **Charge Name** and the **Amount**.
2. Click **Add Another Charge** to add more than one charge.
3. Click **Apply Charges** to apply them, **Clear All Charges** to remove them, or **Cancel** to close the form.
