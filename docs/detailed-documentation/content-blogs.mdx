# Web Presence, Blogs & Subscriptions

A strong web presence and engaging content strategy drives organic user acquisition and brand credibility. This guide covers website appearance settings, content publishing via the built-in blogging engine, and marketplace vendor subscription plans.

---

## 1. General Web Settings (`/general_settings`)

![General Web Settings](/img/detailed-documentation/general-web-settings.webp)

Controls the customer-facing eMarket website header, hero banners, footer navigation, and brand messaging.

### Key Sections Configured
- **Header & Navigation**: Primary logo, navigation links, and customer login buttons.
- **Hero & Promotional Sections**: Tagline, call-to-action app download buttons (Google Play & Apple App Store).
- **Footer Blocks**: About snippet, legal links, customer care links, and copyright text.
- **Color Theme**: Synchronize web storefront accents with brand colors.

---

## 2. Social Media Channels (`/social_media`)

![Social Media](/img/detailed-documentation/social-media.webp)

Direct customers to your verified corporate social channels to build community and trust.

### Setup Instructions
- Add official profile URLs for **Facebook**, **Instagram**, **Twitter / X**, **YouTube**, **LinkedIn**, and **WhatsApp Support**.
- Icons appear dynamically in website footers and app sidebar drawers.

---

## 3. Blog Engine & Content Marketing

Content marketing is one of the highest-ROI channels for attracting organic search traffic to your marketplace.

### 3.1 Blog Categories (`/blog_categories`)

![Blog Categories](/img/detailed-documentation/blog-categories.webp)

Group articles into topical silos such as "Healthy Living", "Recipes", "Tech Gadgets", or "Merchant Spotlight".

---

### 3.2 Publish Blogs (`/blogs`)

![Blogs](/img/detailed-documentation/blogs.webp)

A rich WYSIWYG blogging console for drafting and publishing articles.

#### Publishing Steps
1. Navigate to **Blogs > Add Blog**.
2. Enter the **Article Title** and select the **Blog Category**.
3. Upload a hero **Featured Image**.
4. Draft the article body using rich text formatting, bullet points, and embedded images.
5. Fill in **SEO Meta Keywords** and **Meta Description** to maximize search rankings.
6. Toggle status to `Published`.

---

## 4. Vendor Subscriptions (`/subscriptions`)

![Subscriptions](/img/detailed-documentation/subscriptions.webp)

In addition to order commissions, marketplace operators can monetize vendors through recurring subscription membership plans (Free, Bronze, Silver, Gold).

### Subscription Plan Parameters
- **Plan Name & Pricing**: Monthly or annual fee.
- **Product Limit**: Maximum active SKUs a vendor can list (e.g. 50 products for Bronze, Unlimited for Gold).
- **Order Limit**: Monthly order cap before requiring plan upgrade.
- **Commission Discount**: Preferential lower commission rates for premium subscription subscribers.

### Subscriptions FAQ (`/subscriptions_faqs`)

![Subscriptions FAQs](/img/detailed-documentation/subscriptions-faqs.webp)

Provides vendors with clear answers regarding plan upgrades, billing cycles, automated renewals, and cancellation terms.

---

## Real-World Web & Subscription Use Cases

- **Monetizing High-Volume Merchants**: Instead of charging a 15% commission on every order, high-volume supermarkets subscribe to a ₹4,999/month **Gold Subscription Plan** that cuts their per-order commission down to 5%.
- **SEO Traffic Acquisition via Recipe Blogs**: An online grocery marketplace publishes weekly seasonal recipes (e.g. "Top 5 Summer Smoothies") with direct clickable links to add all recipe ingredients to cart with a single tap.
- **App Store Download Acquisition**: Directing website visitors via prominent **General Web Settings** app badges increases daily mobile app installation volume.
