# Seller & Vendor Management

eMarket is built from the ground up as a multi-vendor marketplace platform. The Seller Management suite enables platform owners to scale their vendor ecosystem, verify merchant identities, enforce commission structures, and maintain transparent financial settlements.

---

## 1. Manage Sellers (`/sellers`)

![Manage Sellers](/img/detailed-documentation/sellers-manage.webp)

The central directory of all active and suspended vendor storefronts on the platform.

### Key Table Columns & Controls
- **Store Name & Logo**: The merchant's branding as seen by shoppers.
- **Owner Name & Contact**: Primary business contact person, email, and verified phone number.
- **Commission Rate (%)**: Platform commission deducted automatically on completed orders.
- **Wallet Balance**: Real-time unsettled vendor earnings available for withdrawal.
- **Product Approval Requirement**: Toggle determining whether the seller's new products require manual admin review before publishing.
- **Store Status Switch**: Instant toggle to suspend or activate a merchant's storefront.
- **Actions**: Edit store settings, view store analytics, view product catalog, or remove vendor.

## 2. Add / Onboard Seller (`/sellers/create`)

![Add Seller](/img/detailed-documentation/sellers-add.webp)

Allows administrators to directly onboard and configure a merchant's store profile, operational zones, and banking information.

### Step-by-Step Onboarding Workflow

1. **Basic Store Information**:
   - Enter **Store Name**, **Seller Name**, **Email Address**, and secure **Password** (for seller panel access).
   - Enter primary **Mobile Number**.
2. **Category & Module Assignment**:
   - Assign the **Module** (e.g., Grocery, Pharmacy, eCommerce).
   - Select the **Permitted Categories** that this vendor is authorized to sell under.
3. **Commission & Approvals Policy**:
   - Set **Admin Commission (%)** (e.g., 10% or 15% platform fee).
   - Configure **Require Product Approval** (Yes/No).
4. **Physical Location & Service Radius**:
   - Enter street address, city, and state.
   - Use the interactive Google Map pin to define the store's exact latitude and longitude for distance-based delivery calculations.
5. **Bank & Tax Details**:
   - Enter Bank Name, Account Holder Name, Account Number, and IFSC / Swift Code for automated payout transfers.
   - Enter Tax / GST / VAT Registration number and National Identity documentation.
6. Click **Save Seller** to activate their account and dispatch their seller panel credentials.

## 3. Seller Registration Requests (`/registered_sellers`)

![Seller Requests](/img/detailed-documentation/seller-requests.webp)

When prospective merchants apply to join the marketplace via the public seller registration portal or partner app, their applications land here for compliance and KYC vetting.

### How Administrators Review Applications
1. Click **View Application** to examine the vendor's submitted business registration, store photos, address proofs, and tax certifications.
2. If verified and compliant, click **Approve** and assign an initial commission tier.
3. If documentation is incomplete, click **Reject** with a clear explanation so the merchant can re-submit the required documents.

## 4. Seller Wallet Transactions (`/seller_wallet_transactions`)

![Seller Wallet Transactions](/img/detailed-documentation/seller-wallet.webp)

An auditable double-entry accounting ledger tracking all financial movements for every vendor across the marketplace.

### Transaction Types Recorded
- **Order Credit**: Net earnings credited to the seller upon successful order delivery (Order Total minus Platform Commission).
- **Admin Debit**: Manual adjustments or chargebacks for customer disputes.
- **Withdrawal Payout**: Deductions when a seller's payout withdrawal request is fulfilled.
- **Subscription Fee**: Automated monthly or yearly fee deductions for marketplace subscription tiers.

## Real-World Multi-Vendor Use Cases

- **Dynamic Merchant Commission Tiers**: A marketplace operator charges a standard 15% commission to general vendors, but offers a preferential 8% commission to a high-volume anchor supermarket using the **Manage Sellers** settings.
- **Enforcing Food Safety Compliance**: For perishable grocery and restaurant vendors, administrators review food safety certifications in **Seller Requests** before granting permission to publish food listings.
- **Vendor Dispute Resolution**: When an item arrives damaged and the customer receives a refund, the administrator uses **Seller Wallet Transactions** to debit the corresponding reimbursement amount from the responsible vendor's balance.
