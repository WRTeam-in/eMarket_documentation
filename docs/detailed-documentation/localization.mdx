# Localization & Geographical Zones

eMarket supports cross-border and multi-regional commerce. This section covers configuring multi-language translations (including RTL support) and defining deliverable geographical zones down to the city and pincode level.

---

## 1. Multi-Language System

eMarket features a dynamic internationalization (i18n) engine supporting English, Arabic, Spanish, French, Hindi, and any custom language.

### 1.1 Manage Languages (`/languages`)

![Manage Languages](/img/detailed-documentation/languages-manage.webp)

Lists all active languages installed across the website and applications.

#### Key Actions & Fields
- **Language Name & Code**: e.g., English (`en`), Arabic (`ar`), Spanish (`es`).
- **Direction Toggle**: Switch between **LTR** (Left-to-Right) and **RTL** (Right-to-Left) for Arabic/Hebrew script rendering.
- **Default Language**: Sets the platform fallback language.
- **Status Switch**: Enable or disable user-facing language selection.

---

### 1.2 Add Language (`/languages/create`)

![Add Language](/img/detailed-documentation/languages-add.webp)

#### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Navigate to **Languages > Add Language**.
2. Enter the **Language Name** (e.g. `Arabic`).
3. Enter the standard **ISO Language Code** (e.g. `ar`).
4. Select text direction (**RTL** or **LTR**).
5. Upload a flag icon.
6. Click **Save Language** to auto-generate the translation key dictionary for that language.

---

## 2. Countries Management (`/countries`)

![Countries](/img/detailed-documentation/countries.webp)

Defines the nations where your platform operates, establishing default international telephone calling codes and currency conventions.

### Parameters Configured
- **Country Name** (e.g., India, United States, United Arab Emirates).
- **Dialing Calling Code** (e.g., `+91`, `+1`, `+971`).
- **Country ISO Code** (`IN`, `US`, `AE`).

---

## 3. Serviceable Cities & Zones (`/cities`)

![Cities](/img/detailed-documentation/cities.webp)

Controls the exact geographic boundaries where vendors are permitted to sell and delivery partners fulfill orders.

### City & Boundary Configuration
- **City Name**: Urban center or municipal region.
- **Geofenced Coordinates**: Map coordinates (latitude, longitude) and radius polygon defining delivery coverage.
- **Associated Pincodes / Postal Codes**: Customer delivery addresses must fall within these active pincodes to complete checkout.

---

## Real-World Localization & Territory Use Cases

- **Right-to-Left (RTL) Arabic Commerce**: When deploying in the Middle East, activating Arabic with the **RTL** flag automatically mirrors the entire mobile app layout, navigation drawers, and typography for native Arabic users.
- **Staged Geographic City Expansion**: A marketplace launching in a new metropolitan area creates a new **City** record with designated launch pincodes, allowing them to onboard local vendors ahead of marketing launch.
- **Blocking Deliveries Outside Service Radii**: If a shopper enters an address outside the defined **Cities** boundary, the app gracefully notifies them that doorstep delivery is not yet available in their neighborhood.
