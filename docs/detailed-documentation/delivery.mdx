# Delivery Operations & Fleet Management

Last-mile delivery is often the most critical touchpoint in on-demand commerce. The Delivery Operations suite in eMarket equips logistics managers to supervise driver fleets, manage scheduled delivery time windows, reconcile cash collected on delivery, and administer driver payroll.

---

## 1. Manage Delivery Partners (`/delivery_boys`)

![Manage Delivery Boys](/img/detailed-documentation/delivery-boys-manage.webp)

The live master roster of all registered delivery personnel operating across your service territory.

### Key Columns & Driver Parameters
- **Name & Avatar**: Driver identity and profile photo.
- **Mobile Number**: Primary contact for dispatch and emergency communication.
- **Cash in Hand (₹)**: Current un-remitted cash collected by the driver from Cash-on-Delivery (COD) orders.
- **Cash Limit (₹)**: Maximum COD cash threshold allowed before the driver is locked from receiving new orders until remitting funds.
- **Bonus / Commission Percentage (%)**: Driver payout incentive per completed delivery.
- **Availability / Status Switch**: Toggle to mark a driver as active or temporarily offline.
- **Actions**: Edit driver profile, view order fulfillment history, or suspend account.

## 2. Onboard Delivery Partner (`/delivery_boys/create`)

![Add Delivery Boy](/img/detailed-documentation/delivery-boys-add.webp)

Form used by dispatch operations to register verified couriers into the dispatch system.

### Step-by-Step Onboarding Guide
1. Enter the driver's **Full Name**, **Email**, **Phone Number**, and initial **Login Password** for the Delivery Partner Flutter App.
2. Enter their residential **Address**, **City**, and assign operational delivery zones.
3. Configure financial parameters:
   - **Cash Limit**: Set threshold (e.g. ₹5,000 maximum COD cash holding).
   - **Bonus Percentage**: Set per-order commission or incentive rate.
4. Upload KYC verification documents:
   - **Driving License** (Front & Back).
   - **National Identity Card** (Government-issued photo ID).
5. Set status to `Active` and click **Save**.

## 3. Delivery Partner Applications (`/registered_delivery_boys`)

![Delivery Boy Requests](/img/detailed-documentation/delivery-boy-requests.webp)

When couriers sign up independently through the Delivery Partner Mobile App, their profiles remain pending here until background verification is finalized.

### Verification Steps
1. Inspect uploaded license copies, vehicle registration documents, and identity numbers.
2. Confirm telephone verification or reference checks.
3. Click **Approve** to activate their account and permit immediate order dispatching.

## 4. Delivery Settings & Time Slots (`/delivery_settings`)

![Delivery Settings](/img/detailed-documentation/delivery-settings.webp)

Configure operational delivery windows, estimated fulfillment lead times, and daily order capacity limits.

### Time Slot Configuration
- **Operating Days**: Select which days of the week delivery operations run.
- **Time Slot Windows**: Create customizable time bands (e.g. `08:00 AM - 11:00 AM`, `01:00 PM - 04:00 PM`, `06:00 PM - 09:00 PM`).
- **Maximum Order Capacity**: Limit orders per time window (e.g., maximum 50 orders per slot) to prevent delivery partner bottlenecks.

## 5. Cash Collection Reconciliation (`/cash_collection`)

![Cash Collection](/img/detailed-documentation/cash-collection.webp)

Tracks the vital financial loop of Cash-on-Delivery (COD) orders, ensuring physical cash collected from customers is properly deposited with the platform cashier.

### Step-by-Step Cash Handover
1. When a delivery partner visits the depot or hub to deposit physical cash:
2. Open **Cash Collection** and search for the driver's name.
3. Review their recorded **Cash in Hand** balance.
4. Enter the physical amount received into the **Collect Cash** prompt.
5. Click **Confirm Collection**. The driver's cash-in-hand liability decreases immediately, unlocking their dispatch queue if they had previously hit their cash ceiling.

## Real-World Logistics Use Cases

- **Preventing COD Cash Loss**: By setting a strict ₹3,000 **Cash Limit**, the platform automatically stops assigning new COD orders to a driver until they remit their collected cash to an authorized hub.
- **Handling Weather / Holiday Capacity Spikes**: During rainy days or holidays, operations managers edit **Delivery Settings** to restrict maximum orders per slot, preventing over-commitment and protecting customer satisfaction.
- **Autonomous Driver Onboarding**: Delivery drivers download the partner app, submit their own license photos via mobile, and administrators verify and approve them within 15 minutes via **Delivery Boy Requests**.
