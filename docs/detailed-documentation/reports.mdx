# Reports & Business Intelligence

Data-driven decision making separates high-performing marketplace operators from the competition. eMarket's reporting suite delivers deep analytics across revenue streams, product velocities, and commission margins.

---

## 1. Overall Sales Reports (`/sales_reports`)

![Sales Reports](/img/detailed-documentation/reports-sales.webp)

A comprehensive macroeconomic view of total platform sales turnover across any chosen timeframe.

### Key Metrics & Visualizations
- **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)**: Total gross sales generated across all categories.
- **Net Revenue**: Revenue retained by the platform after deducting vendor payouts and coupons.
- **Total Tax Collected**: Breakdown of GST / VAT collected across regional jurisdictions.
- **Total Delivery Fees & Surcharges**: Aggregate logistics revenues.
- **Export Capabilities**: Download full datasets in CSV, Excel, or printable PDF formats for accountant reconciliation.

---

## 2. Product Sales Reports (`/product_sales_reports`)

![Product Sales Reports](/img/detailed-documentation/reports-product-sales.webp)

SKU-level performance analytics showing which items are moving fast and which are underperforming.

### Report Columns
- **Product Name & SKU ID**: Product identity.
- **Vendor / Seller**: Store offering the item.
- **Units Sold**: Volume velocity across the selected date range.
- **Gross Revenue (₹)**: Total turnover generated by the SKU.
- **Average Selling Price**: Effective realization after discounts.

### Operational Actions
- Identify hero products to feature in **Home Sliders** and **Featured Sections**.
- Identify slow-moving items to advise vendors on clearance price markdowns.

---

## 3. Commission Reports (`/commission_reports`)

![Commission Reports](/img/detailed-documentation/reports-commission.webp)

An auditable breakdown of all platform commission revenues collected from marketplace vendors.

### Financial Breakdown
- **Seller Name & ID**: Vendor reference.
- **Total Orders Fulfilled**: Number of successful customer deliveries.
- **Gross Order Value (₹)**: Base order total.
- **Agreed Commission Rate (%)**: Contractual fee (e.g. 10%, 15%).
- **Platform Commission Earned (₹)**: Net marketplace fee retained by the platform owner.

---

## Real-World Analytics Use Cases

- **Quarterly Tax Filing**: Accountants use the **Sales Reports** date filter to export all orders for Q1, obtaining exact tax totals for government VAT/GST submissions.
- **Vendor Performance Reviews**: During annual merchant contract renewals, marketplace managers review the **Commission Reports** to evaluate the seller's total turnover and renegotiate commission tiers.
- **Inventory Demand Forecasting**: Evaluating the 30-day **Product Sales Report** allows warehouse managers to accurately forecast demand and restock inventory before popular items run dry.
