# Catalog & Product Management

The Catalog system in eMarket powers product discovery, categorization, stock tracking, and pricing across multi-vendor stores. It provides both high-level governance for marketplace administrators and granular catalog control for individual sellers.

---

## 1. Category Management

Categories structure your store catalog into intuitive, navigable hierarchies that make it easy for shoppers to discover items on mobile apps and the web store.

### 1.1 Manage Categories (`/manage_categories`)

![Manage Categories](/img/detailed-documentation/categories-manage.webp)

Displays the complete hierarchical tree of main categories and nested sub-categories.

#### Key Features & Elements
- **Category Tree Table**: Displays Category Name, Sub-category count, Thumbnail image, Banner image, Status (`Active` / `Inactive`), and Actions.
- **Search & Filter**: Search by category title or filter by parent category.
- **Quick Status Toggle**: Instantly activate or deactivate entire product taxonomies with a single switch.

---

### 1.2 Add Category (`/manage_categories/create`)

![Add Category](/img/detailed-documentation/categories-add.webp)

The form used to create top-level departments or nested child categories.

#### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Navigate to **Categories > Add Category**.
2. Select **Parent Category** (leave empty for a main department, or select an existing parent to create a subcategory).
3. Enter the **Category Name** in the default platform language and any secondary languages configured.
4. Upload a high-resolution square **Category Image** (used on app home screens and grid icons).
5. Upload a wide **Category Banner** (displayed at the top of category product listing pages).
6. Toggle **Status** to `Active` and click **Save**.

---

### 1.3 Categories Order (`/categories_order`)

![Categories Order](/img/detailed-documentation/categories-order.webp)

Allows administrators to define the visual display sequence of categories on the customer mobile app home screen and navigation menus.

#### Operational Instructions
- Drag and drop category rows up or down to set their display priority.
- Categories at the top of this list appear first in customer apps and website mega-menus.

## 2. Product Management

### 2.1 Manage Products (`/manage_products`)

![Manage Products](/img/detailed-documentation/products-manage.webp)

The primary inventory repository listing all active, draft, and out-of-stock products across all sellers.

#### Key Table Fields
- **Product ID & Image**: Visual thumbnail with SKU identifier.
- **Name & Category**: Product title linked to its catalog category.
- **Seller**: The vendor offering the product.
- **Price & Discount**: Base price, special discounted offer price, and active margin.
- **Stock Status**: In-Stock indicator or low-stock warning alert.
- **Status Switch**: Fast toggle to show or hide the product on storefronts.
- **Actions**: Edit, duplicate/clone product, view public page, or delete.

---

### 2.2 Add Product (`/manage_products/create`)

![Add Product](/img/detailed-documentation/products-add.webp)

A comprehensive multi-tab product wizard enabling rich product specifications, variant combinations, media assets, and SEO metadata.

#### Step-by-Step Product Creation Workflow
1. **General Information**:
   - Select **Store / Seller** and choose the primary **Category**.
   - Fill in **Product Name**, **Short Description**, and rich HTML **Full Description**.
   - Set **Brand** and assign applicable **Tax Rules**.
2. **Product Type & Variants**:
   - Choose between **Simple Product** (single SKU) or **Variable Product** (multiple sizes, colors, weights).
   - For variable products, add attributes (e.g., Weight: 500g, 1kg) to auto-generate variant rows with independent pricing and stock numbers.
3. **Pricing & Inventory**:
   - Define **Standard Price**, **Discounted Price**, and **Stock Quantity**.
   - Set **Minimum Order Quantity** and **Maximum Allowed Quantity per Order**.
4. **Media & Gallery**:
   - Upload the main thumbnail and gallery photos via the Media Manager.
5. **SEO & Additional Flags**:
   - Enter Meta Title and Meta Description for search engine indexing.
   - Toggle flags: `Is Returnable`, `Is Cancelable`, and `COD Allowed`.
6. Click **Submit Product** to publish or submit for admin approval.

## 3. Product Approvals (`/approve_requests`)

![Product Approvals](/img/detailed-documentation/approve-requests.webp)

In multi-vendor marketplaces where quality control is enforced, new products and major edits submitted by sellers land here for quality inspection before going live.

### How It Works
1. Review the seller's submitted title, imagery, description, and pricing.
2. Click **Approve** to immediately publish the listing live across customer apps.
3. Click **Reject** and provide a rejection remark (e.g., "Image resolution too low" or "Inappropriate category") so the seller can correct and re-submit.

## 5. Media Library (`/media`)

![Media Library](/img/detailed-documentation/media.webp)

A centralized cloud asset repository for product imagery, category banners, promotional icons, and brand graphics.

### Key Capabilities
- Drag-and-drop batch upload of WebP, PNG, and JPEG files.
- Copy permanent image URLs for instant reuse across sliders and product variants without re-uploading duplicate assets.

## 6. Bulk Operations

### 6.1 Bulk Upload (`/bulk_upload`)

![Bulk Upload](/img/detailed-documentation/bulk-upload.webp)

Onboard hundreds or thousands of products simultaneously using CSV or Excel spreadsheets.

#### Workflow Steps
1. Download the pre-formatted **Sample CSV Template** and review column instructions.
2. Populate product titles, category IDs, seller IDs, prices, variant specs, and image URLs.
3. Upload the completed spreadsheet. The background parser validates each line and imports all items with zero manual data entry.

---

### 6.2 Bulk Update (`/bulk_update`)

![Bulk Update](/img/detailed-documentation/bulk-update.webp)

Export your existing catalog to a spreadsheet, quickly modify prices or stock levels in bulk, and re-import to update your entire inventory in seconds.

## 7. Taxes, Brands, Addons & Attributes

| Section | Route | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Taxes** | `/taxes` | Configure regional tax slabs (e.g. 5% GST, 12% VAT) applied automatically during checkout. |
| **Brands** | `/brands` | Maintain official manufacturer and brand catalogs (e.g. Nestlé, Apple, Nike) for brand-based filtering. |
| **Addons** | `/addons` | Create customizable side items and extras (e.g. extra cheese, gift packaging, cutlery) for Food and specialty modules. |
| **Attributes** | `/attributes` | Define variant attributes such as Size (S, M, L, XL), Color, or Pack Size. |

## 8. Stock Management (`/manage_stock`)

![Stock Management](/img/detailed-documentation/stock-management.webp)

A dedicated, real-time inventory ledger giving store managers an instant overview of inventory health.

### Key Operations
- Spot items with **Low Stock** warnings before they sell out.
- Rapidly adjust stock counts inline without opening full product edit forms.
- Filter by out-of-stock items to generate supplier replenishment purchase orders.

---

## Real-World Catalog Use Cases

- **Seasonal Catalog Refresh**: During festival or holiday sales, store managers use **Bulk Update** to mark down hundreds of SKUs and activate seasonal discounts across entire categories.
- **Vendor Product Quality Assurance**: Large marketplaces require all seller-submitted items to go through the **Approve Requests** pipeline, ensuring every listing has professional photography and complies with regulatory labeling requirements.
- **Fast Stock Reconciliation**: After physical warehouse inventory audits, store teams use **Stock Management** to adjust on-hand counts and prevent overselling out-of-stock variants.
