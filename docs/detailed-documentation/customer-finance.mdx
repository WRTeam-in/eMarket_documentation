# Customer Accounts & Financial Operations

Managing customer accounts, digital wallets, and reverse logistics workflows is essential for customer retention and fraud prevention. This guide details user management, returns processing, and payout reconciliation in eMarket.

---

## 1. Customers Registry (`/users`)

![Customers](/img/detailed-documentation/customers.webp)

The central database of all shoppers registered across the mobile applications and web storefront.

### Key Table Information
- **Customer Name & Avatar**: User profile details.
- **Email & Mobile**: Verified communication channels.
- **Wallet Balance (₹)**: Current digital wallet credits available for order payment.
- **Referral Code**: Unique invitation code for the platform Refer & Earn program.
- **Account Status Switch**: Instant control to deactivate bad-faith or fraudulent customer accounts.
- **Actions**: View complete order history, adjust wallet balances manually, or inspect saved addresses.

---

## 2. Customer Digital Wallet (`/wallet_transactions`)

![Customer Wallet](/img/detailed-documentation/customer-wallet.webp)

Tracks every credit and debit transaction across customer digital wallets.

### Common Wallet Movements
- **Cashback Credits**: Promotional cashbacks rewarded from promo codes.
- **Instant Order Refunds**: Money returned immediately to the customer wallet when an out-of-stock item is cancelled.
- **Order Debit**: Wallet funds deducted during cart checkout.
- **Manual Admin Adjustment**: Customer support crediting goodwill balance to resolve service grievances.

---

## 3. Payment Gateway Transactions (`/transactions`)

![Transactions](/img/detailed-documentation/transactions.webp)

The master payment ledger recording all inbound financial settlements via external payment gateways (Stripe, Razorpay, PayPal, Cashfree, Midtrans, Paytm).

### Transaction Fields
- **Transaction ID & Order ID**: Cross-reference identifiers.
- **Payment Gateway**: Gateway processor name.
- **Amount & Currency**: Settled funds.
- **Status**: `Success`, `Pending`, or `Failed`.
- **Timestamp**: Exact gateway authorization time.

## 4. Return Requests & Reverse Logistics (`/return_requests`)

![Return Requests](/img/detailed-documentation/return-requests.webp)

Handles customer product returns when goods arrive damaged, expired, or incorrect.

### Step-by-Step Return Resolution Workflow
1. When a customer submits a return request through their order history screen in the mobile app:
2. Open **Return Requests** in the admin panel to view the case.
3. Review the customer's stated reason, uploaded photo proofs, and original order invoice.
4. **Approve Request**:
   - Assign a delivery partner for doorstep package pickup.
   - Once the driver confirms package handover at the depot, the system automatically credits the refund back to the customer's wallet or original payment method.
5. **Reject Request**:
   - Enter an explanatory remark (e.g., "Return window expired" or "Item opened/consumed").

---

## 5. Withdrawal Requests (`/withdrawal_requests`)

![Withdrawal Requests](/img/detailed-documentation/withdrawal-requests.webp)

When vendors or delivery partners accumulate earnings in their wallet balances, they submit payout withdrawal requests to cash out into their registered bank accounts.

### Step-by-Step Payout Fulfillment
1. Open **Withdrawal Requests** to view pending cash-out petitions.
2. Check the requester's identity, verified bank account number, and requested payout amount.
3. Execute the electronic bank wire transfer via your banking portal or payment API.
4. Return to the admin panel, enter the **Bank Transaction / UTR Number**, and click **Mark as Approved / Transferred**.
5. The requester's wallet balance updates instantly and a confirmation notification is dispatched.

## Real-World Financial & User Use Cases

- **Immediate Goodwill Credits**: A customer reports a missing item in their grocery order. Support immediately credits the item's value into their **Customer Wallet** within 60 seconds, converting an unhappy customer into a loyal advocate.
- **Auditing Disputed Chargebacks**: When a credit card chargeback notification arrives from Stripe, the finance team searches the **Payment Transactions** table by transaction reference to trace the order, customer IP, and delivery proof.
- **Weekly Vendor Payout Batches**: Finance teams review all verified **Withdrawal Requests** every Monday morning, executing automated batch bank transfers and closing out weekly merchant disbursements cleanly.
