# Features

Browse the newest and most important topics across the eMarket platform — from multi-module operations to mobile app distribution.

:::tip What's New in the Latest Release
- 📍 **Live Delivery Tracking**: Customers watch the delivery boy move on the map in real time, along the road route.
- 🗺️ **OpenStreetMap Support**: Choose free OpenStreetMap or Google Maps for the apps from the Admin Panel.
- 🧾 **One-Screen Cart & Checkout**: Cart and checkout combined into a single screen in the Customer App and Website.
- 🎙️ **Multi-Product Voice Search**: Say several products in one sentence and search for all of them at once.
- 🔎 **Search from Home**: Search products right from the home screen, without opening a new page.
- 🔁 **Out-of-Stock Replacements**: Swap out-of-stock cart items for similar products.
- 🛒 **Abandoned Carts**: Sellers follow up on abandoned carts from the Seller Panel and Partner App.
- 🏙️ **Multi-City Delivery Boys**: Delivery boys can serve more than one city.
- ✨ **Liquid Glass on iOS 26**: The Customer App takes on Apple's Liquid Glass look on iOS 26 and later.
- 🌙 **Redesigned Panels**: Modern Admin, Seller and Delivery Boy panels with dark mode and full RTL support.
- 🚀 **Laravel 12 & Next.js 16**: Backend and Website upgraded for performance, security and long-term support.
:::
