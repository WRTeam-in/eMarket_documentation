# Features

Browse the newest and most important topics across the eMarket platform — from multi-module operations to mobile app distribution.

:::tip What's New in the Latest Release
- 📍 **Real-Time Delivery Boy Tracking**: Customers can follow the delivery boy's live location on the map.
- 🏙️ **Multi-City & Admin/Seller-Wise Delivery Boys**: Delivery boys can serve multiple cities and be managed separately by the admin and each seller.
- 🛒 **Abandoned Cart Management**: Sellers can view and follow up on abandoned carts from the Seller Panel and Partner App.
- 🗺️ **OpenStreetMap Integration**: An alternative map provider to Google Maps across the Admin Panel, Customer App, and Partner App.
- ⏱️ **Quick Delivery Arrival Countdown**: A live countdown to the expected arrival of Quick Delivery orders.
- 🎙️ **Multi-Product Voice Search**: Search for several products at once by voice, or straight from the home screen.
- 🔁 **Similar Product Replacement**: Out-of-stock cart items can be swapped for similar products.
- 💳 **Single-Screen Cart & Checkout**: Cart and checkout combined into one screen in the Customer App and website.
- 🖥️ **Redesigned Panels**: A modern, responsive Admin, Seller, and Delivery Boy panel UI with dark mode, Arabic RTL, and Card/Table views.
- 🍎 **Liquid Glass for iOS 26**: Customer App support for the new iOS 26 Liquid Glass design.
- 🚀 **Laravel 12 & Next.js 16**: Backend upgraded to Laravel 12 and website to Next.js 16.2.4 with server-side rendering for SEO on VPS.
- ⚡ **Quick Delivery (15 & 30 Minutes)**: Ultra-fast hyper-local fulfillment options for rapid everyday essentials.
- 🍔 **Food Delivery Module**: Dedicated vertical with restaurant menus, dish addons, modifiers, and instant courier dispatch.
- 🛠️ **Seller-Level Attributes & Custom Fields**: Independent product attribute and custom field creation directly from the seller panel.
- 🎨 **Module-Wise Theme Color Management**: Distinct primary branding colors and accents per business vertical (Grocery, Food, Pharmacy, etc.).
- 🖼️ **Next-Gen WebP Image Support**: Native WebP compression across the Admin Panel and Customer Website for ultra-fast loading.
- 🌐 **Website Social Media Management**: Manage external social media links and channel icons directly from the Admin Panel.
- 🥗 **Dietary Markers (Veg / Non-Veg)**: Moved to dynamic custom fields for flexible dietary classification and allergen tagging.
- 📱 **Flutter 3.47.2 Modern Codebase**: Upgraded Customer and Partner apps for 120Hz smooth scrolling and latest OS security.
- ⚠️ **Seller Migration Alerts**: Automatic warnings for legacy global attributes to guarantee smooth vendor migration.
:::
